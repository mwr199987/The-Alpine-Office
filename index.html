01 / Choosing well

A slope-side address.
A better family week?

An illustrative planning consideration

The address alone cannot answer that. We look at the route to ski school, the terrain outside the door and how everyone will get home.

A quieter chalet with a reliable driver may suit a family better than a celebrated slope-side property. We would explain that trade-off before recommending either.

The recommendation follows the people.
